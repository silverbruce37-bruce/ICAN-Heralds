MANILA – The Philippines and South Korea officially inaugurated the 'PH-Korea Digital Innovation Hub' today, a state-of-the-art facility designed to accelerate technological advancements and foster a vibrant startup ecosystem in the region. Located in Clark Freeport Zone, Pampanga, the hub represents a strategic partnership aimed at leveraging both nations' strengths in innovation and digital transformation. Officials from both governments emphasized the hub's potential to drive economic diversification and create high-value jobs.

The initiative, which has been years in the making, includes incubation programs for emerging tech companies, specialized training for digital professionals, and collaborative research and development projects. Funding for the hub comes from a joint investment, with significant contributions from the Korean Ministry of Science and ICT and the Philippine Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT). Industry leaders and venture capitalists from both countries have also pledged support, signaling strong confidence in the hub's future impact.

During the opening ceremony, President Marcos Jr. highlighted the hub as a testament to the enduring friendship and deepening economic ties between the Philippines and Korea. Ambassador Lee Sang-hwa echoed these sentiments, stating that the project will serve as a beacon for digital cooperation within ASEAN, attracting further foreign investment and talent. The first cohort of startups is expected to commence operations by Q4 2026, focusing on areas such as AI, fintech, and sustainable technology.
