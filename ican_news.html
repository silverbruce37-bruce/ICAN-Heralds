Manila, Philippines – A high-level delegation from South Korea, led by Minister of Science and ICT, concluded a productive two-day Digital Partnership Summit in Manila yesterday, May 19th. The summit culminated in the signing of several key Memoranda of Understanding (MOUs) aimed at fostering deeper collaboration in critical digital sectors. Philippine President and the Korean Minister emphasized the shared vision of leveraging digital transformation for national development and regional stability.

Among the significant agreements reached was a comprehensive plan for joint research and development in artificial intelligence and blockchain technologies, alongside initiatives to strengthen cybersecurity frameworks in the Philippines. Korea has pledged technical assistance and expertise to help the Philippines accelerate its digital transformation agenda, particularly in developing smart cities and enhancing public services through digital platforms. This move is seen as a crucial step towards bridging the digital divide and improving connectivity.

The agreements are expected to create numerous opportunities for Korean businesses in the Philippines, especially those in IT, telecommunications, and software development. Furthermore, the enhanced digital cooperation is anticipated to improve the ease of doing business for Korean expatriates and companies operating in the archipelago, ensuring a more secure and efficient digital environment. Both parties expressed optimism that these partnerships would not only drive economic growth but also deepen the cultural and social ties between Korea and the Philippines.
