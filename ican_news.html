A leading South Korean technology conglomerate, Innovatech Korea, officially broke ground today on its ambitious new innovation hub in Clark Global City, Pampanga. The state-of-the-art facility, spanning over 50 hectares, is envisioned to become a regional center for artificial intelligence research, software development, and digital incubation, attracting both local and international talent.

Philippine President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. and South Korean Ambassador to the Philippines, H.E. Lee Sang-hwa, graced the ceremony, emphasizing the project's strategic importance. President Marcos highlighted the investment's potential to significantly boost the Philippines' digital economy, foster technological transfer, and provide high-skilled employment opportunities for Filipino youth. Ambassador Lee underscored the enduring partnership between the two nations, positioning this hub as a cornerstone for future collaborative ventures in the digital realm.

Innovatech Korea's CEO, Kim Min-joon, expressed confidence that the innovation hub will serve as a catalyst for growth, driving innovation not just within the company but across the entire ASEAN region. The facility is expected to be fully operational by late 2028, with initial phases focusing on R&D and talent development programs that will commence early next year.
