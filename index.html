New Clark City, Philippines – The Philippines and South Korea officially launched a groundbreaking smart city partnership today, August 1, 2026, marking a significant milestone in bilateral cooperation. The initiative, dubbed the 'PH-Korea Smart Urban Development Project,' will leverage Korean expertise in smart infrastructure and digital solutions to transform New Clark City into a model of sustainable and technologically advanced urban living. Officials from both nations emphasized their commitment to creating a future-ready metropolis that addresses the challenges of rapid urbanization through innovative strategies.

The project encompasses several key areas, including intelligent transportation systems, energy-efficient building designs, advanced waste management, and comprehensive digital connectivity. Korean technology firms will collaborate closely with Philippine counterparts, facilitating knowledge transfer and capacity building for local engineers and urban planners. This partnership is expected to attract substantial foreign investment, creating numerous job opportunities and stimulating economic growth not only in Central Luzon but across the wider Philippine economy, positioning the region as a hub for smart technology innovation.

Beyond technological advancements, the 'PH-Korea Smart Urban Development Project' also prioritizes community welfare and environmental sustainability. Plans include the integration of green spaces, smart public services, and disaster resilience infrastructure, ensuring a high quality of life for residents. Both governments expressed optimism that this collaboration will serve as a blueprint for future smart city developments in ASEAN, further strengthening the strategic alliance between the Philippines and South Korea and demonstrating their shared vision for a prosperous and technologically empowered future.
