Manila, Philippines – In a landmark move poised to redefine the digital landscape of Southeast Asia, the Philippines and South Korea officially inaugurated the 'PH-KR Digital Innovation Hub' yesterday. Located in Bonifacio Global City, the hub is envisioned as a vibrant ecosystem designed to nurture local startups, facilitate technology transfer, and foster joint research and development initiatives, ultimately driving job creation and economic diversification in both nations.

The initiative, a direct outcome of the recent bilateral economic dialogues, focuses on key areas such as Artificial Intelligence, FinTech, cybersecurity, and smart city solutions. Funding will be jointly provided by both governments, alongside private sector investments, ensuring robust support for promising ventures. Experts believe this collaboration will not only enhance the Philippines' digital capabilities but also provide Korean tech companies with an agile gateway to the broader ASEAN market.

Officials from both countries expressed optimism regarding the hub's potential to serve as a catalyst for regional digital transformation. Philippine Secretary of Trade and Industry, Alfredo Pascual, highlighted the hub's role in empowering local talent, while Korean Ambassador to the Philippines, Lee Sang-hwa, emphasized its significance in deepening the strategic partnership between the two nations beyond traditional trade, moving towards a future-oriented digital economy.
