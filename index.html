Clark Freeport Zone, Philippines – The Philippines and South Korea today officially launched the 'PH-Korea Digital Innovation Hub' in Clark, Pampanga, a collaborative project designed to accelerate digital transformation and foster a vibrant technology ecosystem in the region. The inauguration ceremony was attended by high-ranking officials from both nations, including the Philippine Secretary of Trade and Industry and the Korean Ambassador to the Philippines, highlighting the strategic importance of this partnership.

The state-of-the-art facility will serve as a training center for digital skills, a research and development hub for emerging technologies, and an incubator for tech startups. It aims to address the growing demand for skilled professionals in areas such as AI, cybersecurity, and data analytics, providing opportunities for both Filipino and Korean tech talents. This initiative is expected to attract further foreign direct investment into the Philippine tech sector and create thousands of new jobs.

Beyond economic benefits, the Digital Innovation Hub is poised to strengthen the enduring bilateral relationship between the Philippines and South Korea, fostering cultural exchange and shared learning. It underscores a mutual commitment to advancing the digital economy and leveraging technological innovation for sustainable development across ASEAN. Korean businesses operating in the Philippines are encouraged to utilize the hub's resources and contribute to its growth, further cementing the bond between the two countries' economies.
